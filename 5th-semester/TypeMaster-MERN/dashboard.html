Typing Tips

01

Maintain Proper Posture

Sit straight, keep your feet flat on the floor, and position your keyboard at elbow height.

02

Use All Fingers

Place your fingers on the home row keys (ASDF - JKL;) and use all fingers for typing.

03

Don't Look at Keyboard

Keep your eyes on the screen to improve muscle memory and typing speed.

04

Practice Regularly

Consistent practice, even 15 minutes daily, yields better results than long sessions.
